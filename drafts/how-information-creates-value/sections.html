How Information Creates Value

From Individual Experiences to Health Intelligence

Every participant contributes valuable insight into how healthcare works in the real world.

The information collected through Health Studies may be combined with other authorized data sources to better understand:

  • What healthcare challenges people are facing
  • Why people delay or avoid care
  • Barriers to medication access
  • Benefit affordability concerns
  • Healthcare navigation difficulties
  • Emerging healthcare trends

Rather than focusing on isolated experiences, PSM identifies large patterns that can help improve healthcare systems, benefits, and services.
